Dragon Feast

Cover Art

Dragon Feast is a single-player rogue-like game about a Dragon breaking into a castle to retrieve its eggs while def/-eating all his opponents along its way. Players can expect a fast-paced combat system, an artsy medieval setting and a goofy dragon.

Character Art

Dragon feast was my 3rd Semester Project where I worked in a group of four. In this Project I did most of the UV unwrapping but my main task was Programming, I made the Character Controller, Random Level Generation, Fight Mechanics, Enemy AI...

All You Can Eat

Excerpt of the Print Document

At the end of my second Semester I worked in a group of four on a Physical Game Project.

For this project we developed All You Can Eat, a fast paced strategical Card Game.

Box Print

“All you can eat” is a card game themed around the foodchain, represented by cards of animals fighting each other. The ultimate Goal of the players is to get rid of all their cards, they utilise various abilities to make it easier for themselves to lose more cards and other abilities to make it harder for the other players to do so.

In this Project I worked on the digitalization of scetches and coloring them. Further more I helped designing core game Mechanics. At last I created the Print Document
